01 / About

I test the path between a requirement and a reliable release.

My foundation is manual QA: understanding the risk, exploring the edge cases, and making quality visible to the team.

I work across functional, exploratory, regression, integration, and UAT testing. My day-to-day practice includes requirement analysis, test-data preparation, positive and negative scenarios, cross-module validation, evidence collection, reproducible defect reporting, retest, and regression readiness.

I’m now deepening that foundation with Playwright, TypeScript, REST API testing, SQL, and maintainable test design—without losing sight of the product and the people using it.
